by Bertrand Dalimier (Independent Researcher) for publication as a regular research article in *Linear Algebra and its Applications*.

The paper studies a finite family of mutually orthogonal projections ((P_i)_{iin I}) summing to the identity and a unitary operator (U). It compares the quadratic cellwise commutator budget

Second, for positive defect it characterizes equality exactly: saturation occurs if and only if precisely two cells have nonzero commutator defect. Third, it proves a dimension-free inverse stability theorem showing that near saturation forces quantitative concentration of the quadratic defect on two cells, with the explicit modulus

The manuscript is positioned as a finite-dimensional matrix/operator result rather than as an approximation-to-a-commuting-tuple theorem. The projections and the unitary are kept fixed; the inverse result concerns the internal structure of the existing commutator profile in the near-equality regime. This places the work within the journal’s scope in matrix theory and finite-dimensional linear algebra, while connecting naturally with classical work on projections, commutator inequalities, invariant subspaces, and almost commuting matrices.

A companion Lean 4 development formally verifies the principal inequality, equality classification, quantitative modulus, rank-one formula, explicit sharpness family, and asymptotic obstruction. The manuscript describes this formalization as a reproducibility and verification artifact; the mathematical contribution is the operator-theoretic result itself.
